- The deck will begin with war-wands instead of swords. Each war-wand has its own mana pool and spell it could cast
	- Spice ball
	- Snow storm
	- Gust
	- Cure wounds
- The war wands do 1 damage and gain mana on attack similar to units.

- Green Tea is a consumable card that can be used on a unit or spell to make it gain 2 mana on hit instead of 1.

- Haste X: a spell that applies X stacks the haste status. The status counts down every round and give the card 1 frenzy while its applied.
