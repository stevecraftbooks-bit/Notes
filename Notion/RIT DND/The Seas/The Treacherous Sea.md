> > [!note]+ ## Aquark
> > - The capital city of this sea. The city specializes in shipwrights and their main export is tidal fish. A type of fish that group in large schools, have small edible bones and are light and refreshing.
> > - The city has a magical metal that forms beneath its surface that many call aquarkum. Not many know the properties of this material, but it is said to be very expensive and highly volatile.
> > - The city is in turmoil, the government wants to sink the city but the people don’t want to. 
> > - There are no workers making guarding places harder. The miners of the city have all stopped doing their jobs. So new research has stopped. The front city guards are working for a lot of money from the shipwrights.
> > - The mayor of the city is a kraken flople. He knows the city used to be underwater, and wants to sink it back down. He knows that if the people get a hold of aquarkum it could be bad for the sea people. 
> > - Traveling through the city reveals a bustling amount of people, however the more you look at them, there is a great divide amongst the poor and the rich. 
> > - The rich people are seen wearing silken garb while the poor are just walking, talking and spreading rumors. “The mayor has gone mad”. “We are going into a recession”. “We didn’t ask for a dictator”.
> > - There are more guards surrounding special buildings located around the city. Each equipped with shell phones. 
> > - Theas buildings are the entrances to the mines where they recently found the aquarkum. The mayor is trying to stop the people from seeing it, but it’s causing the recession.
> > - The only way in would be to sneak in through some other entrance or kill the guards.
