*The user blindly pulls a bean out of the bag and looks at its color. The user then decides whether they want to eat or toss the the bean depending on the effect referenced below:*

### **1. Red Beans**

- **Positive Effect**: The bean grants the player temporary strength. For the next hour, the player gains advantage on Strength checks and saving throws.
- **Negative Effect**: The player becomes temporarily reckless. For the next hour, they have disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws and AC drops by 2.

### 2. **Blue Beans**

- **Positive Effect**: The player gains enhanced intelligence. For the next hour, they gain a +2 bonus to Intelligence checks and saving throws.
- **Negative Effect**: The player becomes mentally drained. For the next hour, they suffer disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws and have a -2 penalty to Intelligence checks.

### 3. **Green Beans**

- **Positive Effect**: The player is granted a burst of natural vitality. They regain 2d6 hit points immediately and gain advantage on Constitution saving throws for the next hour.
- **Negative Effect**: The player experiences severe fatigue. They lose 2d6 hit points immediately and have disadvantage on all ability checks for the next hour.

### 4. **Yellow Beans**

- **Positive Effect**: The player is blessed with heightened speed. They can Dash as a bonus action and gain +10 feet to their movement speed for the next hour.
- **Negative Effect**: The player becomes clumsy. Their movement speed is reduced by 10 feet, and they have disadvantage on Acrobatics checks for the next hour.

### 5. **Purple Beans**

- **Positive Effect**: The player gains a mystical aura. For the next hour, they gain the ability to cast *Counterspell* once without expending a spell slot.
- **Negative Effect**: The player’s magical energies are unstable. For the next hour, they suffer a -2 penalty to all spell attack rolls, and any concentration checks are made with disadvantage.

### 6. **Black Beans**

- **Positive Effect**: The player is imbued with shadowy power. For the next hour, they can cast *Invisibility* once without expending a spell slot.
- **Negative Effect**: The player becomes overwhelmed by darkness. For the next hour, they are unable to see in bright light and have disadvantage on attack rolls in sunlight.
