> [!note]+ Explode metal:
> Choose any non-magical metal object within range that you can see. The object explodes in a 10ft radius.

> [!note]+ Circuit Sawblades:
> Fire 3 sawblades that reduce the targets AC by 1.

> [!note]+ Orbit object
> Choose any object within range you can see. A copy of that object will orbit 5ft from you. This object can interact with anything like normal and can be pulled away from the orbit, but tries to re-enter orbit when possible. Requires concentration.

> [!note]+ Summon plant golem
> Summons a high AC plant golem that can cast some plant form of chromatic orb. Requires Concentration.

> [!note]+ Tall jump
> The user can jump up to 100ft in the air and land as of nothing happened once.
